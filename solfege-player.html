Solfège Player

Load a hymn, choose a key, and hear it sung back in do re mi, one voice or the whole choir.

84 bpm

Most hymns already exist as clean notation. Download a .musicxml, .mxl, or .mid file and load it here: best quality, instant, full SATB voices, no scanning.

Free sources: CPDL (choral) · Hymnary · MuseScore

Type a melody by hand

One note per token: letter + optional #/b + octave, e.g. C4, F#5, Bb3. Add a duration with a colon (4=quarter, 8=eighth, 2=half): G4:8. Use R for a rest.

Paste MusicXML

Paste the contents of a .musicxml / .xml file. All voices and staves are parsed (Soprano/Alto/Tenor/Bass).

Transcribe a photo (local OMR)

For sheet music you can't find as a file. Choose an image or paste a screenshot anywhere with ⌘V. Needs the local backend running. See backend/README.md (uvicorn main:app --port 8000). OMR is imperfect on dense SATB, so always check the result.

Show all parts as

Each part shows movable-do solfège or its own engraved staff, drawn from your notes. Toggle one part, or all at once above. Use the Edit tab to fix or build parts.

Save score

The whole score, drawn from your notes. Compare it against your PDF. The cursor follows playback. (MIDI has no notation.)
